## Core Services

### LiaScript Consulting & Integration

Advice on introducing LiaScript in universities, schools, NGOs and companies, from the first pilot to rollout across the organisation.
This includes integrating LiaScript into your existing learning environments and Learning Management Systems, for example through [SCORM, xAPI or IMS exports](/exporter/).

### Course Creation & Migration

Turning your existing material, such as PowerPoint presentations, PDFs, Markdown documents and other teaching resources, into interactive, reusable LiaScript courses.

### Training & Workshops

Hands-on training for teachers, developers and educational institutions: how to write, publish and further develop interactive courses with LiaScript, either on site or online.

### Custom Development

Custom LiaScript extensions, macros and [templates](/categories/template/), plus visualizations, simulations and interactive learning components tailored to your subject and audience.
